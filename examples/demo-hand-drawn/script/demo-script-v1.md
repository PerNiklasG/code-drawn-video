(Pebblesprout is a fictional app made up for this example.)

0:00 — HOOK
VISUAL: A two-week wall calendar draws on under a "MORNING RUN" label. Day one gets a big coral tick and
sparkles; days two to eight tick fast; day nine gets a question mark and the calendar droops, ticks greying.
You've started a new habit before. Day one feels great. By day nine, it's gone.

0:07 — PROBLEM
VISUAL: A phone with a streak counter counting up to 47, a flame and a row of day dots. The last dot is crossed
out, the counter drops to 0 with a shake and the flame turns grey. The phone is put away. Music drops out.
Most habit apps count streaks. Miss one day, and the counter drops to zero. So you quit.

0:14 — MECHANISM
VISUAL: Logo: a pebble pops in, a sprout grows out of it, wordmark "Pebblesprout". Three habit cards; one is
circled. A big pebble is tapped (ripple), a sprout grows; a week card fills its first dot. A skipped day shows
a waiting ring with a "z", and the sprout keeps growing and flowers. Then a whole garden of pebbles sprouts.
Pebblesprout works the other way round. Pick one small habit. Each day you do it, tap your pebble, and a little
sprout grows. Skip a day, and it simply waits for you. Nothing resets. Nothing dies. Over a few weeks, your
pebbles become a garden you can see.

0:32 — PROOF
VISUAL: A growth line that only goes up, with a flat stretch labelled "BREAK" where a streak would have dropped
to zero; leaves pop along it, and more leaves burst at the end on "green".
Because the garden only grows, every day counts, even after a break. No guilt. No reset button. Just a little
more green.

VISUAL: A phone showing "My garden", then the logo, wordmark and "Free on your phone", sparkles.
