# Style note — Pebblesprout demo

Direction chosen: **action-comic** (from three proposed: action-comic, kinetic-type, flat-motion).

- Why: a 30-second app ad that has to stop a scroll; the product is playful, the claim is simple, so loud
  colour fields, slam titles and comic effects carry it without detailed illustration.
- Colour: hot yellow / red / blue halftone fields per section (hook / problem / mechanism), black outlines,
  green for the sprouts.
- Type: Bangers for slam titles and kinetic words, Rubik for numbers and UI.
- Motion: punchy (fast overshoot), camera shake, whip / flash / punch / glitch / iris transitions.
- Sound: electronic-drive at 126 BPM, whooshes on transitions, a boom on "boom", pops and clicks on taps.
- Voice: Kokoro `af_bella` at speed 1.1; a real ad would use an energetic cloud voice or a recorded read.
